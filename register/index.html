Welcome to Pack 615New Scouts: register and pay

  1. Join Scouting America

    $160 paid to Scouting America

    Membership and the national fee are handled on Scouting America’s own site. The unit should read Pack 0615 Melrose Highlands Congregational Church, and the application is for a child or youth. The adult options are for volunteers.

    An invitation to Scoutbook follows by email. That is where badges and progress are recorded.

    Register with Scouting America (opens in a new tab)

  2. Pay Pack 615’s activity fee

    $145*

    The pack’s own fee, covering badges, the Pinewood Derby car kit, campsite fees and materials for meetings. One per Scout.

    Scout Life magazine is offered during the Scouting America application in step 1, at $15.

    Pay the activity fee for new Scouts, opens Square in a new tab

  3. Hand in the health forms

    Scouting America requires a paper health record before a child joins in. Forms A, B1 and B2 signed by hand, with copies of an immunization record and insurance card, in a sealed envelope to your den leader.

    Anyone coming on a weekend trip needs one, adults included.

    Download the health forms (PDF, opens in a new tab)

Welcome backReturning Scouts: renew and pay

  1. Renew for 2026-2027

    $305*

    Covers the $160 Scouting America membership and the $145 pack activity fee. The pack renews the membership on your behalf. One per Scout.

    Scout Life magazine is offered at checkout, at $15 for the year.

    Already renewed the membership yourself? Then only the pack’s activity fee is due, so use the new Scout activity fee (opens Square in a new tab) instead, which is pack fees only despite the name.

    Renew and pay the returning Scout fee, opens Square in a new tab

  2. Hand in the health forms

    Scouting America requires a paper health record before a child joins in. Forms A, B1 and B2 signed by hand, with copies of an immunization record and insurance card, in a sealed envelope to your den leader.

    Anyone coming on a weekend trip needs one, adults included.

    Download the health forms (PDF, opens in a new tab)

Scouting America sends its own renewal emails. The pack handles the renewal, so they can be ignored.

The Big Trip

One night inside a museum. The highlight of the pack year.

EcoTarium sleepover, Worcester

$70 per person coming

Friday January 8 at 5:30pm to Saturday January 9, 2027 at 9:30am. This year’s theme is Stars & Space: the planetarium, a trip to Mars, launching a rocket, and a look through a telescope if the sky is clear. An evening snack and a continental breakfast are included, and so is free admission to come back another day.

Each Scout is accompanied by one adult, so most families need two tickets. No siblings. Tickets are limited, non-refundable, and separate from the activity fee.

Donate

  1. Donate to the financial aid fund

    You choose the amount

    When a family picks $25, $50 or $75 off at checkout, this fund covers the difference. It is given by Pack 615 families and neighbours, and it pays for fees, uniforms and activities.

Volunteering as an adult

Optional.

  1. Register as an adult volunteer

    $80 the pack pays you back

    Adults in a named role, such as a den leader, register with Scouting America as well. The fee covers a background check and insurance, and the pack reimburses it.

    Coming along to meetings and events needs none of this. On the signup it is the “applying for myself” option, or “both” alongside a child.

    Register as a volunteer (opens Scouting America in a new tab)

There is no hurry. Most people come along for a while first.
