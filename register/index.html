Welcome to the pack!New Scouts: register and pay

Two payments and one form, in this order.

  1. Join Scouting America

    $160 paid to Scouting America

    This registers your child as a Cub Scout. It goes to Scouting America, not to the pack.

    Choose “I’m applying for a child / youth”. The other two options are for adults, and you do not need either one to sign your child up. See volunteering below. Check the unit says Pack 0615 Melrose Highlands Congregational Church before you pay.

    Afterwards you will get an email inviting you to Scoutbook. Accept it. That is where your child’s badges and progress are recorded, and where you can see what they are working towards.

    Register with Scouting America (opens in a new tab)

  2. Pay Pack 615’s activity fee

    $145*

    This is the pack’s own fee, and it covers what your child actually does through the year: the badges they earn, the kit for the Pinewood Derby car race, campsite fees, and the odds and ends for meetings. Pay it once for each Scout in your family.

    Note: the Scout Life magazine is not offered here. New families opt in during the Scouting America application in step 1, for $15.

    Pay the activity fee for new Scouts, opens Square in a new tab

  3. Hand in the health forms

    Required before your child takes part in anything, including the first meeting. Print forms A, B1 and B2, then fill them in and sign them by hand.

    Put those, a copy of your child’s immunization record and your insurance card in a sealed envelope, and give it to your den leader.

    One set for everyone coming on a weekend trip, not just your Scout. That means you too.

    Download the health forms (PDF, opens in a new tab)

Welcome back!Returning Scouts: renew and pay

One payment and one form. The pack renews your Scouting America membership for you.

  1. Renew for 2026-2027

    $305*

    One payment covers both things: the $160 Scouting America membership, which the pack renews on your behalf, and the pack’s $145 activity fee. There is nothing for you to do on the Scouting America site. Pay this once for each Scout in your family.

    Optional: the checkout also offers Scout Life, Scouting America’s magazine for kids, mailed to your house, at $15 for the year. Choosing “no subscription” changes nothing else.

    Already renewed your child’s membership yourself? Then do not pay this. You owe only the pack’s activity fee, so use the new Scout activity fee (opens Square in a new tab) instead. It is pack fees only, despite the name.

    Renew and pay the returning Scout fee, opens Square in a new tab

  2. Hand in the health forms

    Required before your child takes part in anything, including the first meeting. Print forms A, B1 and B2, then fill them in and sign them by hand.

    Put those, a copy of your child’s immunization record and your insurance card in a sealed envelope, and give it to your den leader.

    One set for everyone coming on a weekend trip, not just your Scout. That means you too.

    Download the health forms (PDF, opens in a new tab)

Scouting America will email you about renewing. Ignore it. The pack renews your child’s membership on your behalf, and it is already covered by the payment above.

The Big Trip

One night inside a museum. The highlight of the pack year.

EcoTarium sleepover, Worcester

$70 per person coming

Friday January 8 at 5:30pm to Saturday January 9, 2027 at 9:30am. This year’s theme is Stars & Space: the planetarium, a trip to Mars, launching a rocket, and a look through a telescope if the sky is clear. An evening snack and a continental breakfast are included, and so is free admission to come back another day.

Every Scout needs an adult with them, so most families buy two tickets. One adult per family, and no siblings. Tickets are limited and non-refundable, and this is not covered by the activity fee.

Donate

The reduced rates on this page are paid for by other families. This is that money.

  1. Donate to the financial aid fund

    You choose the amount

    When a family picks $25, $50 or $75 off at checkout, this fund covers the difference. It is money given by Pack 615 families and neighbours, and it pays for fees, uniforms, and activities so that a kid whose family cannot manage the full price still joins, still camps, and still gets the shirt.

Volunteering as an adult

Optional, and separate from signing your child up.

  1. Register as an adult volunteer

    $80 the pack pays you back

    Adults who take on a volunteer role register with Scouting America too. The fee covers a background check and insurance. Pack 615 reimburses it, so volunteering does not cost you money.

    You do not need this to sign your child up, and you do not need it to come along to meetings and events. It is only for a named volunteer role, like a den leader. On the same signup, it is the “I’m applying for myself” option, or “both” if you are doing your child at the same time.

    Register as a volunteer (opens Scouting America in a new tab)

Not sure whether you want a role yet? Nothing here is urgent. Come to a few things first, then talk to us.
